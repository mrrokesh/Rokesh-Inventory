# Quotes (estimates) and delivery challans

## Estimates: send a quote before the order

An **estimate** (also called a quote or quotation) tells a customer what something will cost. It doesn't change your stock or money. If the customer agrees, you turn it into a sales order with one click.

1. Go to **Sales → Estimates → + New**.
2. Choose the customer, add the items and prices, and set an **Expiry date** (how long the price is valid).
3. Press **Save and Mark as Sent**. Use **Email** or **Print / PDF** to send it.
4. When the customer replies, open the estimate and press **Mark accepted** (or **More → Mark declined**).
   - If the customer uses the [customer portal](/help/customer-portal), they can accept or decline it themselves.
5. Press **Convert to sales order**. A sales order is created with the same items and prices — confirm it and continue as usual ([Selling](/help/selling)).

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not sent yet. |
| Sent | Waiting for the customer. Shows as *Expired* after the expiry date. |
| Accepted / Declined | The customer's answer. |
| Converted | A sales order has been created from it. |

## Delivery challans: sending goods without a tax invoice

A **delivery challan** is a document that goes with goods when you send them **without selling them yet**. Common reasons:

- **Supply on approval** – the customer tries the goods first and buys only what they keep.
- **Job work** – you send material to someone to work on (repair, assembly) and it comes back.
- **Others** – moving goods to a site, exhibition, demo, etc.

### Create and dispatch

1. Go to **Sales → Delivery Challans → + New**.
2. Choose the customer, the **Challan type**, and add the items.
3. Press **Save and Open (stock out)**. The goods leave your stock now.
4. Press **Print / PDF** to print the challan to send with the goods.
5. When they arrive, press **Mark delivered** (optional).

### Then one of two things happens

- **The customer keeps the goods** → press **Convert to invoice**. The invoice does *not* take the stock out again — the challan already did.
- **The goods come back** → choose **More → Mark goods returned**. Everything goes back into stock.

> **Tip:** Only part kept? Mark the challan returned (all stock comes back) and then create a normal invoice for what the customer kept.
