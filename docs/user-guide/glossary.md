# Words explained

Plain-language meanings of every term you will see in the app, in alphabetical order.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Adjustment** | A correction to your stock count, for example after counting the shelves or finding damaged goods. |
| **Assembly** | Building a kit (composite item) from its parts. Assembling 5 kits removes the parts from stock and adds 5 kits. |
| **AWB** | *Air Waybill* – the courier's tracking number for a shipment. |
| **Available for sale** | Stock on hand minus committed stock. This is what you can still promise to new customers. |
| **Balance due** | The part of an invoice or bill that has not been paid yet. |
| **Batch** | A group of units made together, usually with a batch number and expiry date. See [Serial numbers and batches](/help/serial-batch). |
| **Bill** | The invoice a *vendor* sends you. Recording it tells the app you owe them money. |
| **CGST / SGST / IGST** | Parts of GST. Sales within your state charge CGST + SGST (half each); sales to another state charge IGST. See [GST](/help/gst). |
| **Committed stock** | Stock that is already promised to customers on confirmed sales orders but has not been shipped yet. |
| **Composite item (kit / bundle)** | An item made of other items, for example a "Laptop kit" made of a laptop, a mouse and a bag. |
| **Confirm** | Turns a draft sales order into a real order. The items become *committed*. |
| **Cost price** | What you pay your vendor for one unit. |
| **Credit note** | A document that reduces what a customer owes you, usually after a return. You can apply it to an invoice or refund the money. |
| **Customer** | A person or business you sell to. |
| **Customer portal** | A private website where your customers sign in to see their own invoices, orders and payments. |
| **Delivery challan** | A document that goes with goods sent without a tax invoice (on approval, job work). |
| **Estimate (quote)** | A price offer to a customer. It can be converted into a sales order. |
| **Draft** | A saved document that has no effect yet. Safe to edit or delete. |
| **FIFO** | *First In, First Out.* When you sell, the app assumes the oldest stock leaves first, and uses that stock's purchase price to work out your cost and profit. |
| **GST** | Goods and Services Tax (India). Only needed if your business is GST-registered. |
| **GSTIN** | Your 15-character GST registration number. |
| **HSN / SAC code** | Government codes that classify goods (HSN) and services (SAC) for GST. |
| **Invoice** | The bill you send to your customer asking them to pay. |
| **Issue** | Turns a draft purchase order into a real order sent to your vendor. |
| **Item** | Anything you buy or sell – a product (goods) or a service. |
| **Item group** | A family of similar items that differ by size, colour or model, for example a T-shirt in S, M and L. |
| **Opening stock** | The quantity you already had before you started using the app. |
| **Overdue** | An invoice or bill whose due date has passed and is still not fully paid. |
| **Package** | The box you pack for a customer. Packing doesn't change stock yet; shipping does. |
| **Payment received** | Money a customer paid you. |
| **Payment made** | Money you paid a vendor. |
| **Payment link** | A web link where a customer can pay an invoice online (UPI, card, net banking). |
| **Payment terms** | How many days the customer has to pay, for example *Net 15* = 15 days. *Due on receipt* = immediately. |
| **Place of supply** | The state where goods or services are delivered. Decides CGST + SGST or IGST. |
| **Price list** | Special prices for some customers or vendors, for example 10% off for wholesale buyers. |
| **Purchase order (PO)** | Your order to a vendor asking them to supply goods. |
| **Purchase receive** | Recording that the goods from a purchase order have arrived. Stock goes **up**. |
| **Reorder point** | When available stock falls to this number, the app warns you to buy more. |
| **Sales order (SO)** | A customer's order that you have accepted. |
| **Sales return** | Goods a customer sends back. |
| **Selling price** | What you charge a customer for one unit. |
| **Serial number** | A unique number printed on a single unit, like a laptop's serial number. |
| **Shipment** | Recording that a package has left your warehouse. Stock goes **down**. |
| **SKU** | *Stock Keeping Unit* – your own short code for an item, for example `LAP-DELL-5440`. Must be unique. |
| **Stock on hand** | How many units are physically in your warehouses right now. |
| **Track inventory** | When ticked, the app counts stock for the item. Untick it for services or things you don't count. |
| **Transfer order** | Moving stock from one warehouse to another. |
| **Unit** | How you count an item: pcs, box, kg, licence, hours… |
| **Vendor** | A supplier you buy from. |
| **Vendor credit** | A document that reduces what you owe a vendor, for example after returning faulty goods. |
| **Void** | Cancels a posted document. Its effect on stock is reversed, but the document stays in history marked *Void*. |
| **Warehouse** | Any place where you keep stock – a godown, shop, or office. |

## What changes stock?

| Action | Stock |
|---|---|
| Opening stock, purchase receive, sales return received, transfer in, assembly (kit) | ⬆ goes up |
| Shipment, direct invoice (without a sales order), open delivery challan, transfer out, vendor credit with returned goods, assembly (parts) | ⬇ goes down |
| Confirming a sales order | Stays the same, but becomes *committed* |
| Drafts, estimates, packages, purchase orders, bills linked to a purchase order, invoices made from a delivery challan | No change |
