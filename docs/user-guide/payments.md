# Payments

## Record money a customer paid

1. Go to **Sales → Payments Received → + New** (or press **Record payment** on an invoice).
2. Choose the **Customer**. Their unpaid invoices appear below.
3. Type the **Amount received**.
4. Choose the **Payment mode** – Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Card…
5. Type a **Reference#** (UTR number, cheque number, transaction ID) so you can match it with your bank statement later.
6. Under each invoice, enter how much of the payment goes to it – or press **Apply amount automatically** to pay the oldest invoices first.
7. Press **Save**.

If the customer paid more than they owe, the extra is kept as **Amount in excess** (an advance) and shows as *unused credit* on their page.

## Record money you paid a vendor

Same steps under **Purchases → Payments Made → + New** (or **Record payment** on a bill).

## Print a receipt

Open the payment and press **Print receipt**.

## Fix a wrong payment

Open the payment and press **Edit** to change the amount or which invoices it pays, or **Delete** to remove it. The invoices go back to unpaid.

## Bank charges

If your bank or payment gateway deducted a fee, enter it in **Bank charges** on the payment received.
