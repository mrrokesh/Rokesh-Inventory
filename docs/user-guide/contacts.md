# Customers and vendors

**Customers** are the people and businesses you sell to. **Vendors** are your suppliers. Both are added the same way.

## Add a customer

1. Go to **Sales → Customers → + New**.
2. Choose **Business** or **Individual**.
3. Fill in the **Primary contact** name and **Company name** if it's a business.
4. **Display name** is the name shown on documents and lists. Pick one of the suggestions or type your own.
5. Add an **Email** and **Phone** so you can reach them.
6. Open the tabs below to add more (all optional):
   - **Other Details** – GST treatment and GSTIN, PAN, payment terms (how many days they get to pay), credit limit, price list.
   - **Address** – billing and shipping addresses. Use **Copy billing address** if they're the same.
   - **Contact Persons** – other people at the company.
   - **Remarks** – private notes only your team sees.
7. Press **Save**.

Adding a vendor is identical: **Purchases → Vendors → + New**.

## GST treatment (India)

Only important if you are GST-registered.

| Choose | When the customer/vendor… |
|---|---|
| Registered Business – Regular | has a normal GSTIN |
| Registered Business – Composition | is registered under the composition scheme |
| Unregistered Business | is a business without GST registration |
| Consumer | is an individual buying for personal use |
| Overseas | is outside India |
| Special Economic Zone | is in an SEZ |

## Payment terms and credit limit

- **Payment terms** set the invoice due date automatically. *Net 30* means the customer must pay within 30 days.
- **Credit limit** is the most a customer may owe you. When a new order would take them over it, you'll see a warning (you can still save it).

## The customer page

Open any customer to see:

- **Outstanding receivables** – how much they owe you right now.
- **Unused credits** – credit notes or extra payments you can use against future invoices.
- **Transactions** – all their sales orders, invoices, payments and credit notes in one place.
- Quick buttons: **New sales order**, **New invoice**, **Receive payment**.

## Import

Use **Import** on the Customers or Vendors list to upload a spreadsheet. Download the template first so the column names match.

## Remove a customer or vendor

You can only delete contacts with no transactions. For everyone else, press **Mark inactive** – they're hidden from new documents but their history is kept.
