# Customer portal

The customer portal is a private website for **your customers**. Each customer signs in with their own email and password and sees only their own information:

- Their **invoices**, how much they owe, and what's overdue
- Their **orders**, **quotes** (which they can accept or decline) and **credit notes**
- **Shipments** with tracking links
- **Payments** they've made and a full **account statement**
- A place to **write messages** to you on any invoice, order or quote
- A **Pay online** button on unpaid invoices (if [Razorpay is connected](/help/email-integrations))

It saves you from answering "can you resend the invoice?" and "where's my parcel?" calls.

## Invite a customer

1. Make sure the customer has an **email address** (Edit customer).
2. Open the customer and find the **Customer portal** box.
3. Press **Invite to portal**.
   - If [email is set up](/help/email-integrations), the invitation is emailed to them automatically.
   - Otherwise an invitation link is copied — paste it into an email or WhatsApp message to the customer.
4. The customer opens the link, chooses a password and is signed in.

The box shows **Invited** until they sign in for the first time, then **Active** with the date they last signed in.

**Forgot password?** Press **Send a new invitation link** on the customer — they use it to choose a new password.

**Stop access:** press **Turn off**. They can no longer sign in.

## Your portal address

Every organization has its own portal address, shown in the invitation link — it looks like `yourapp.com/portal/a1b2c3d4e5f6`. Customers can bookmark it.

## What customers can and can't see

- They see only **their own** documents — never other customers'.
- They never see **drafts**, your costs, stock levels, internal notes or other staff information.
- On any document you can add an **internal note** (tick *Internal note*) that the customer can't see, or a **reply** that they can.

## Messages from customers

When a customer writes a message, it appears in the **Comments** box on that document (marked *customer*) and in **Recent activity** on your dashboard. Reply in the same box — untick *Internal note* so they can see your reply.

## Customers can also

- Update their **phone number and addresses** (name, email and GSTIN can only be changed by you).
- Change their own password.
- **Print or save a PDF** of any invoice or statement.
