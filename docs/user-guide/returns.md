# Returns, credit notes and refunds

When a customer sends something back, there are two separate questions:

1. **The goods** – do they go back on the shelf? → handled by a **sales return**.
2. **The money** – does the customer get money back or a discount on the next bill? → handled by a **credit note**.

## 1. Create a sales return

1. Open the sales order the goods were sold on.
2. Choose **More → Create sales return**. (Only items that were shipped can be returned.)
3. Enter the quantity coming back for each item and a **Reason** (e.g. *Damaged in transit*).
4. Untick **Back to stock** for anything damaged that should **not** be sold again.
5. Press **Create return**.

## 2. Receive the returned goods

When the parcel arrives back, open the sales return and press **Receive items**. The items marked *Back to stock* are added to your stock again.

## 3. Give the customer credit

1. On the sales return, press **Create credit note**. The items and prices are filled in for you.
2. Press **Save as Open**.

Now choose what to do with the credit:

- **Apply to invoices** – reduces the balance of an unpaid invoice. Use this if the customer hasn't paid yet or will buy again.
- **Refund** – record that you paid the money back (cash, UPI, bank transfer…).

You can split a credit note: apply part, refund the rest.

## A discount without a return

If you only want to give money back (for example, a late-delivery discount) without any goods coming back, open the invoice and choose **More → Create credit note**, adjust the amount, and press **Save as Open**.

## Undoing a return

Delete or void the credit note first, then open the sales return and press **Delete**. Restocked items are removed again.
