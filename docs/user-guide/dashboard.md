# Using the Home dashboard

The Home page is made of **widgets** – small cards that each answer one question at a glance, such as *"What is running low?"* or *"How much do customers owe me?"*

## Reading a widget

- The **big number** is the answer. Smaller text underneath explains it.
- A **⚠ symbol and red text** means something needs your attention (for example, overdue money).
- A **✓ symbol and green text** means everything is fine.
- **Click a widget** to open the full list behind it. For example, clicking *Low stock* opens the list of items that need reordering.
- Some widgets have a button you can use right there – *Reorder* on Low stock, or *Delivered* on On the way.

The time under "Hello" shows when the numbers were last updated. The dashboard refreshes itself every 5 minutes and whenever you come back to the tab. Press **Refresh** to update it immediately.

## Choosing the period

Use the drop-down at the top right (*This month*, *This fiscal year*, …) to change the period for sales, payments and charts. Stock and money owed are always shown as of right now.

## Customizing your dashboard

Everyone can arrange their own dashboard – your changes don't affect other users.

1. Press **Customize** at the top right.
2. To **move** a widget, drag it to a new place, or use the **◀ ▶** buttons on it.
3. To **change size**, press **S** (small), **M** (medium) or **L** (large). Larger sizes show more detail.
4. To **remove** a widget, press **×**.
5. To **add** a widget, press **Add widgets**, then pick a size under the widget you want.
6. Press **Done**. Your layout is saved automatically.

Press **Reset** (while customizing) to go back to the standard layout.

## The widgets

| Widget | Answers the question |
|---|---|
| Getting started | What should I set up next? |
| Today | What happened today – orders, invoices, payments, shipments? |
| Low stock | Which items should I reorder? |
| Sales activity | Which orders are waiting to be packed, shipped, delivered or invoiced? |
| Purchase activity | Which purchase orders are waiting to be received or billed? |
| Stock value | How much is my stock worth? |
| Money owed to you | How much do customers owe me, and how late is it? |
| Money you owe | How much do I owe vendors? |
| Cash in & out | Did more money come in than went out? |
| Sales by month | Are sales going up or down? |
| Best sellers | What sells the most? |
| On the way | Which shipments haven't been delivered yet? |
| Top vendors | Whom do I buy from the most? |
| Sales by channel | How much of my sales comes from each channel (direct, Shopify…)? |
| Receive history | What did vendors deliver most recently? |
| Recent activity | What did my team just do? |

> **Tip:** You only see widgets for the parts of the app your role is allowed to use.

## Light and dark appearance

Click your initials (top right) and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Match system**.
