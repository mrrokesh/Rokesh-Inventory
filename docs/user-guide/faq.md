# Questions & problems

## "Not enough stock for … in Primary Warehouse"

You're trying to ship, invoice, transfer or adjust out more than you have in that warehouse.

- Check the item page – maybe the stock is in **another warehouse**. Pick that warehouse or transfer the stock.
- Receive the purchase order for the goods first.
- If the opening stock was never entered, add it (item page → **More → Add opening stock**) or make an adjustment.

## "Enter 2 serial number(s) for …" or "Batch quantities add up to …"

The item is tracked by serial number or batch. Press **Add serial numbers** / **Add batch details** under the item and enter exactly as many as the quantity. See [Serial numbers and batches](/help/serial-batch).

## "Email is not set up yet"

An administrator needs to connect an email account in **Settings → Email** ([how](/help/email-integrations)). Until then, use **Print / PDF** and attach the PDF to your own email.

## A customer can't sign in to the portal

Open the customer and press **Send a new invitation link** in the *Customer portal* box. They use the new link to choose a password. Check that the email address on the customer is correct — it's their sign-in name.

## I can't edit an order or invoice

Only **drafts** can be edited. Once something is confirmed, sent, issued or opened, it has changed your stock or balances. To fix it:

1. Remove anything that depends on it (payments on an invoice; packages and invoices on a sales order).
2. Press **More → Void**.
3. Use **More → Clone** to create a corrected copy.

## I can't delete an item, customer or vendor

They've been used in transactions, and deleting them would break your history. Mark them **inactive** instead – they disappear from new documents.

## I can't delete a purchase receive / transfer

The stock it brought in has already been sold or moved. Reverse the later transactions first, or correct the difference with an **adjustment**.

## The price on an order is different from the item's price

The customer probably has a **price list** assigned (Customer → Edit → Other Details → Price list). You can always type a different rate on the order line.

## Where did my stock go?

Open the item → **Stock history**. Every movement is listed with the document that caused it. Click the reference to open that document.

## Why does the sales order still say "Confirmed" after shipping?

A sales order closes only when everything is **both shipped and invoiced**. Check the badges next to the status (e.g. *Partially invoiced*) to see what's left.

## How do I send an invoice to a customer?

If email is set up, open the invoice and press **Email**. Otherwise press **Print / PDF**, choose **Save as PDF**, and attach it to an email or WhatsApp message. Customers with [portal access](/help/customer-portal) can also see and download it themselves.

## I forgot my password

Ask your administrator to open **Settings → Users** and press **Password reset link** next to your name (it's emailed to you if email is set up). If you are the only administrator and you're locked out, whoever installed the app can help reset it on the server.

## A button I need is missing

Your role may not allow that action. Ask an administrator to check **Settings → Roles & permissions**.

## Is my data saved automatically?

Yes. Every time you press **Save** (or any action button), it is stored on the server immediately. Your dashboard layout and appearance choice are saved too.

## Still stuck?

Press the **?** button at the top of any screen to open the guide for that screen, or browse all guides in [Help & Guides](/help).
