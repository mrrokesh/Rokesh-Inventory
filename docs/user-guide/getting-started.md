# Start here: your first day

This guide walks you through setting up the app and completing your first sale, one step at a time. You do not need any accounting or inventory experience.

## What this app does

Think of the app as a notebook that never makes mistakes. It keeps track of:

- **What you sell** – your products and services (called *items*).
- **How many you have** – how much stock is sitting in each of your warehouses or shops.
- **Who you buy from** – your suppliers (called *vendors*).
- **Who you sell to** – your *customers*.
- **Money** – who owes you, whom you owe, and what has been paid.

Every time you buy, sell, pack, ship or return something, the app updates the stock count and the money for you.

> **Tip:** If you ever see a word you do not understand, open [Words explained](/help/glossary). Every term used in the app is explained there in plain language.

## The screen at a glance

- **Left menu** – the main sections: Home, Items, Inventory, Sales, Purchases, Reports, Documents and Help.
- **Search bar (top)** – type any name, SKU, order number or invoice number to jump straight to it.
- **Blue + button (top)** – create something new from anywhere (an item, a customer, an order…).
- **Bell (top)** – reminders such as items running low or orders waiting to be packed.
- **? button (top)** – opens the help page for the screen you are on.
- **Your initials (top right)** – your profile, light/dark appearance and sign out.

## Set up in 10 steps

Follow these in order. The **Getting started** card on your Home page ticks each one off as you go.

1. **Fill in your company details.** Go to [Settings → Organization profile](/settings/organization). Add your address, phone and logo. These appear on the invoices and orders you print.
2. **Add your taxes (if you charge tax).** Go to [Settings → Taxes](/settings/taxes) and add each rate, for example *GST18* at *18%*. If you are not registered for GST, skip this step.
3. **Check your warehouse.** A warehouse called *Primary Warehouse* already exists. If you keep stock in more than one place (a shop and a godown, for example), add the others in [Settings → Warehouses](/settings/warehouses).
4. **Add your items.** Go to [Items → + New](/items/new). Type the name, the price you sell it for and the price you buy it for. If you already have some in stock, type the quantity under *Opening stock*. Have many items? Use [Import](/import/items) to upload them from a spreadsheet.
5. **Add a vendor.** Go to [Purchases → Vendors → + New](/vendors/new) and add a supplier you buy from.
6. **Add a customer.** Go to [Sales → Customers → + New](/customers/new).
7. **Order stock.** Create a [purchase order](/purchase-orders/new) to your vendor and press **Save and Issue**.
8. **Receive the stock.** When the goods arrive, open the purchase order and press **Receive**. Your stock count goes up.
9. **Sell.** Create a [sales order](/sales-orders/new) for your customer and press **Save and Confirm**. Then press **Create package**, then **Ship package**. Your stock count goes down.
10. **Get paid.** Press **Create invoice** on the sales order, then **Mark as sent**, then **Record payment** when the customer pays.

That's the full cycle. Everything else in the app is a variation of these steps.

## Drafts are safe

Most documents can be saved as a **Draft** first. A draft does not change your stock or money – it is just a note. You can edit or delete a draft as many times as you like. Once you *confirm*, *issue*, *send* or *open* a document, it becomes real and changes your stock or balances.

> ⚠ **Posted documents can't be edited.** If a confirmed document has a mistake, **void** it (or delete it) and create a new one. Voiding puts the stock back exactly as it was.
